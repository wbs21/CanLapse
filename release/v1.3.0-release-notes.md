Timelapse recorder for drawing-app canvas windows — Windows 10/11 x64, single-file executable, no DLLs, no runtime.

## What's new in v1.3.0

- **Timestamped export filenames** — `timelapse.mp4` → `timelapse_YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.mp4`.
  Previously a fixed name combined with "auto-clean cache after successful export" could **silently overwrite** your first export, and the cache was already gone. The timestamp is taken at stop time (matching the video content) and sorts naturally; re-exports of history sessions keep the `<session>_30fps.mp4` naming.
- **Floating control bar appears on the canvas monitor** — it now snaps to the top-right of the monitor where the canvas window lives (`MonitorFromWindow` + `MONITOR_DEFAULTTONEAREST`, primary monitor as fallback), instead of always the primary screen. It can still be dragged along the top edge.
- Removed the unused `App::out_path` field; version resources bumped to 1.3.0.0.

Full history: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/wbs21/CanLapse/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)

## 中文说明

**v1.3.0 更新内容**

- **导出文件名带本地时间戳**：`timelapse.mp4` → `timelapse_20261018-120334.mp4`。
  修复固定文件名 + 「合成成功后自动清理缓存」导致第二次导出**静默覆盖**第一次且缓存已删、不可恢复的问题；时间戳在停止时取（与视频内容一致），重新导出历史会话仍用 `<会话名>_30fps.mp4`。
- **悬浮控制条出现在画布所在的显示器**：用 `MonitorFromWindow` 取画布窗口所在显示器的工作区并贴其右上角，取不到时回退主屏；仍可沿上边缘左右拖动。
- 清理无用的 `App::out_path` 字段；exe 版本资源升至 1.3.0.0。

**运行要求**：Windows 10 / 11 x64，启用 DWM（默认开启）。无需任何运行库——无 DLL、无 .NET、无 VC++ 运行库。
